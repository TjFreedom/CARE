PRF Microneedling in Newark, DE

Care Esthetics Delaware offers consultations for microneedling with platelet-rich fibrin (PRF). Talk with our Newark team about your skin concerns, the proposed procedure, and whether this approach is appropriate for you.

Understand the two parts of treatment

Microneedling uses fine needles to make small punctures in the skin. PRF is prepared from a sample of your own blood. They are distinct parts of a treatment plan: ask your provider to explain the device, how PRF would be used, and the evidence for the proposed combination.

A consultation for your skin

Bring questions about texture, the appearance of acne scars, or facial lines. Your provider will review your skin, prior treatments, medicines, and relevant medical history. Care also lists microneedling with hyaluronic acid among its services; discuss how the proposed options differ instead of assuming they have equivalent benefits.

Plan for the visit and recovery

Ask about preparation, comfort measures, the possible need for a blood draw, expected recovery, and the number of visits recommended for your goals. Get written aftercare instructions, including when to resume your usual skincare. Your provider should explain what to expect and which symptoms warrant contacting the practice.

Limits and safety

FDA authorization of a microneedling device is specific to its indicated uses. The FDA has not authorized microneedling devices to deliver blood products, cosmetics, or other products into the skin. A device's clearance does not establish clearance of a PRF combination. Risks include redness, bleeding, infection, and pigment changes. Discuss your suitability and the uncertainties with your provider.

Common questions

Is PRF the same as PRP?

No. Both are prepared from a person's blood, but the preparations differ. Ask your provider which preparation is proposed and why; this page does not claim one is proven superior.

Will I need several appointments?

That depends on your concerns and the proposed plan. Ask for the expected visit schedule, total estimate, and a review point before beginning treatment.

Can I book if I am unsure which skin treatment I need?

Yes. Request a consultation and describe your goals to the team. You can discuss microneedling, facials, and peels before deciding whether to proceed with a treatment.
