Chemical Peels in Newark, DE

Considering a chemical peel? Start with a skin consultation at Care Esthetics Delaware in Newark to discuss the options on our menu and understand the preparation and recovery involved.

Book a ConsultationCall 302-283-9898

Explore the current menu

Care lists Lite, Sal-e, and Mal-e peels. A peel uses a chemical solution to exfoliate the skin; the exact formulation and treatment depth matter. Ask your provider which product is proposed, what it is intended to address, and whether it is suitable for your skin.

Tell us about your skin

Discuss your usual skincare products, previous peels, recent treatments, sensitivities, and history of unwanted pigment changes. Bring questions about the appearance of uneven tone, texture, or congestion. The consultation is an opportunity to understand what a peel may and may not accomplish for your concerns.

Know what to expect

Ask about the appointment length, comfort, visible peeling, time away from usual activities, and when to expect a review. Recovery varies with the specific treatment and your skin. Before proceeding, discuss possible irritation, pigment changes, and other risks with your provider, and make sure you know how to reach the practice afterward.

Build the plan around your schedule

If you have a trip, photos, or a special event coming up, mention it before scheduling. Request instructions for products and sun exposure before and after treatment. Confirm the price and whether a series is being proposed so that you understand the full plan before deciding.

Common questions

Which peel should I book?

Contact Care if you are unsure. The appropriate selection depends on your skin and the specific product, rather than the service name alone.

Will my skin visibly peel?

The amount of visible peeling varies. Ask about the exact peel being considered and its expected recovery instead of assuming all peels have the same downtime.

Can I combine a peel with another treatment?

Do not assume services should be combined in one visit. Discuss timing, compatibility, and aftercare with your provider before scheduling multiple treatments.

Treatment decisions require an individual consultation. Results vary. This page provides general information and does not replace advice from your treating provider.
