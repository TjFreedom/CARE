Custom Facials in Newark, DE

Make time for your skin at Care Esthetics Delaware. Our Newark facial menu includes options for a quick refresh, hydration, or a more focused conversation about your skincare routine.

Book a ConsultationCall 302-283-9898

Choose a starting point

Our current menu includes express, brightening, acne-focused, anti-aging, hydrating, and teen facials, along with dermaplaning. If the choices feel overwhelming, contact the team with your main concern and ask which appointment to book. A service name is a starting point; the visit should reflect your skin and preferences.

Talk through your routine

Share the products you use, recent professional treatments, sensitivities, and anything that has irritated your skin. Explain whether your priority is a relaxing visit, a simpler routine, or addressing a particular concern. Your provider can discuss the proposed steps and whether another type of appointment would be more appropriate.

What about dermaplaning?

Care lists dermaplaning as a separate menu option. It uses a blade for surface exfoliation and removal of fine facial hair. Ask whether it fits your skin, what is included in the appointment, and how it would relate to any other service you are considering.

Before your appointment

Confirm the treatment duration and price when booking. Ask about any preparation or changes to your usual products, and mention if you are scheduling around an event. After your visit, request a straightforward home-care plan and a way to contact the team if you have questions. Results and skin responses vary.

Common questions

Do you offer an express facial?

Yes, an express facial appears on Care's service menu. Check with our team for current availability, the appointment length, and what is included.

Can a teen schedule a facial?

Care lists a teen facial. Contact the office to confirm age requirements, guardian participation, and which appointment best fits the teen's needs.

Should I choose a facial or a peel?

Tell the team about your goals and current routine. A peel is a different service with its own preparation and recovery considerations. You can review our chemical peel page before your consultation.

Treatment decisions require an individual consultation. Results vary. This page provides general information and does not replace advice from your treating provider.
