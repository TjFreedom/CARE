Dermal & Lip Fillers in Newark, DE

Whether you want to discuss lip shape or changes in facial volume, begin with a plan that considers your whole face. Our Newark team offers dermal filler consultations centered on your preferences and individual anatomy.

What can a filler consultation cover?

Care lists hyaluronic acid dermal fillers among its injectable services. Depending on the specific product and approved treatment area, fillers can add volume or soften certain folds. Ask your provider about the lips, cheeks, or other areas that concern you, and the evidence and limitations for the proposed use.

A plan specific to you

Your consultation is the time to explain the changes you hope to see, show any previous treatment records, and discuss your health history. Your provider should explain product selection, treatment areas, alternatives, and whether it makes sense to treat now or wait. Treatment is optional; asking questions is part of making an informed choice.

Before you book treatment

Ask for the product name, total estimate, expected swelling and recovery, follow-up arrangements, and contact instructions for unexpected symptoms. Do not assume the first appearance after treatment is the final result. Duration and the ability to adjust a result vary by product, treatment area, and individual response.

Risks to discuss

Swelling and bruising are common filler risks. Less common complications include infection or nodules. Accidental injection into a blood vessel can cause tissue damage, vision loss, or stroke. Fillers are medical procedures, and a consultation should include a clear discussion of these risks and your provider's response plan.

Common questions

Can I ask specifically about lip filler?

Yes. Let the team know that lip shape or volume is your concern. The consultation will help determine whether a particular filler and treatment approach are appropriate.

Are all fillers reversible?

No. Some hyaluronic acid fillers can be treated with an enzyme, but removal is not always simple or complete. Other filler materials may be difficult or impossible to remove. Discuss the exact product before treatment.

Is filler the same as Botox?

No. Fillers add material beneath the skin; neurotoxins temporarily reduce muscle activity. Visit our Botox and Dysport page to compare the consultation options.
