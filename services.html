Injectables

Restore Volume & Refresh Your Look

Our expert injectors use the most advanced products to deliver subtle, natural-looking results that enhance — never alter — your natural beauty.

Sculptra

A bio-stimulator that gradually replaces lost collagen, restoring facial volume and improving skin quality over time for results that can last up to 2 years.

  • Gradual, natural-looking results
  • Stimulates your own collagen
  • Lasts up to 2+ years

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid-based fillers to restore volume to the lips, cheeks, jawline, and under-eye areas. Results are immediate and can last 12–18 months.

  • Lips, cheeks, jawline & more
  • Immediate visible results
  • Reversible with hyaluronidase

Neurotoxins (Botox/Dysport)

Smooth dynamic wrinkles including forehead lines, crow's feet, and frown lines. Also used for brow lifting, jaw slimming, and hyperhidrosis.

  • Results visible in 3–7 days
  • Lasts 3–4 months
  • No downtime required

Regenerative Treatment

Microneedling + PRF

Microneedling creates controlled micro-injuries in the skin to trigger your body's natural healing response. When combined with Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) — derived from your own blood — we amplify those results dramatically.

PRF is the next evolution of PRP therapy. It contains higher concentrations of growth factors and fibrin, which slow the release of those factors for longer-lasting results.

Treatment Options:

Microneedling with PRF

100% natural growth factors from your own blood. No additives. Maximum regeneration.

Microneedling with HA

Hyaluronic acid infusion during microneedling for deep hydration and plumping effects.

Resurfacing

Chemical Peels

Chemical peels accelerate skin cell turnover, revealing fresher, smoother, more radiant skin beneath. We offer multiple depths and formulations.

Lite Peel

A gentle superficial peel ideal for first-timers or those with sensitive skin. Minimal downtime with a subtle glow.

  • ✓ Minimal to no peeling
  • ✓ Great for all skin types
  • ✓ No downtime

Sal-e Peel

Salicylic acid-based peel targeting acne-prone and congested skin. Unclogs pores and controls oil production.

  • ✓ Ideal for oily/acne-prone skin
  • ✓ Pore minimizing
  • ✓ 2–4 days downtime

Mal-e Peel

Malic acid-based medium peel for brightening, anti-aging, and texture improvement. Excellent for dry or mature skin.

  • ✓ Anti-aging & brightening
  • ✓ Smooths texture
  • ✓ 3–5 days downtime
