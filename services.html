Regenerative Treatment

Microneedling + PRF

Microneedling uses fine needles to make small punctures in the skin. Care offers consultations for microneedling with PRF, which is prepared from your own blood. Discuss the proposed procedure, expected recovery, and limitations with your provider.

PRF and PRP are different blood-derived preparations. Ask your provider about the evidence for the recommended approach; a microneedling device clearance does not establish FDA authorization for use with blood products.

Treatment Options:

Microneedling with PRF

PRF is prepared from your own blood. Ask about the proposed use, possible benefits, and limitations.

Microneedling with HA

Discuss the proposed hyaluronic acid product, how it would be used, and the limitations of combination treatment.

Resurfacing

Chemical Peels

Chemical peels accelerate skin cell turnover, revealing fresher, smoother, more radiant skin beneath. We offer multiple depths and formulations.

Lite Peel

A gentle superficial peel ideal for first-timers or those with sensitive skin. Minimal downtime with a subtle glow.

  • ✓ Minimal to no peeling
  • ✓ Great for all skin types
  • ✓ No downtime

Sal-e Peel

Salicylic acid-based peel targeting acne-prone and congested skin. Unclogs pores and controls oil production.

  • ✓ Ideal for oily/acne-prone skin
  • ✓ Pore minimizing
  • ✓ 2–4 days downtime

Mal-e Peel

Malic acid-based medium peel for brightening, anti-aging, and texture improvement. Excellent for dry or mature skin.

  • ✓ Anti-aging & brightening
  • ✓ Smooths texture
  • ✓ 3–5 days downtime
