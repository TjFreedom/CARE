PRF Technology

Signature Treatment

The Power of PRF

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is prepared from a sample of your own blood. Ask our providers how it may fit into an aesthetic treatment plan, what evidence supports the proposed use, and what limitations and risks to consider.

  • 100% natural — derived from your own blood
  • No synthetic chemicals or additives
  • Stimulates collagen and elastin production
  • Long-lasting, progressive results
  • Used in microneedling and injectable treatments
