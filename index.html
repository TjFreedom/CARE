PRF Technology

Signature Treatment

The Power of PRF

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is the next generation of regenerative medicine. Unlike synthetic treatments, PRF is derived entirely from your own blood — concentrating your body's natural growth factors to accelerate healing and collagen production.

  • 100% natural — derived from your own blood
  • No synthetic chemicals or additives
  • Stimulates collagen and elastin production
  • Long-lasting, progressive results
  • Used in microneedling and injectable treatments
