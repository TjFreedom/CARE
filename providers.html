Our Providers

Experienced, licensed, and passionate about helping you look and feel your best. Meet the people behind Care Esthetics Delaware.

Lead Provider

Jenine McCloskey

Nurse Practitioner

Jenine McCloskey is a board-certified nurse practitioner with a distinctive background in emergency medicine and trauma. She earned her bachelor's degree from Ohio University before pursuing a Master's degree as a Family Nurse Practitioner from Wilmington University. The majority of her career has been dedicated to emergency medicine, where she honed her critical thinking skills, developed excellent patient communication, and built a strong foundation in anatomy and precise medication administration. With years of experience in high-pressure situations and a deep understanding of patient care, she has transitioned her expertise into the field of aesthetics, focusing on helping individuals feel confident and beautiful.

As a mother of two daughters, Jenine understands the importance of self-care in enhancing well-being, reducing stress, and improving overall quality of life for both individuals and their families. She believes in the philosophy that prioritizing self-care allows individuals to give more generously to those around them.

Having treated tens of thousands of patients, she has developed a deep appreciation for compassion, precision, empathy, and quick thinking—qualities that she integrates into her aesthetics practice every day. She is committed to providing a nurturing and comforting environment where clients feel relaxed and confident in their journey toward enhancing their natural beauty.

Jenine approaches each aesthetic procedure with meticulous attention to detail and a dedication to delivering safe and effective results. She strives to create personalized treatment plans tailored to the unique aesthetic needs and desires of each individual.

Passionate about empowering her clients to feel their best, both inside and out, Jenine fosters open communication and a client-centered approach to build trust and meaningful relationships. Her goal is to go beyond surface-level enhancements to truly impact how her clients feel about themselves.

With a strong foundation in emergency medicine and a deep passion for aesthetics, Jenine brings a holistic approach to beauty and wellness. She is dedicated to helping clients embrace their natural beauty and feel confident in their own skin, working collaboratively to create looks that reflect their inner radiance and empower them to shine.

Specialties:

Injectables, PRF, Facial Aesthetics, Regenerative Medicine

Carly Garrett

Care Coordinator & Esthetician

Carly is a dedicated licensed aesthetician with two years of experience, fully trained in laser and microneedling treatments. She excels in providing both medical-grade and relaxing facials, combining technical expertise with a passion for helping clients achieve their best skin. Her passion for health and self-care led her to the world of esthetics, where she believes that when you look good, you feel good.

Before transitioning into skincare, Carly worked as a dental assistant, which honed her attention to detail—an asset she brings to every skincare consultation. She loves guiding patients toward better skin health and has additional experience in overall wellness. Whether through advanced facials, personalized skincare regimens, or wellness consultations, Carly is dedicated to helping her clients achieve their healthiest, most radiant selves.

Outside of work, Carly enjoys spending quality time with her friends and family, soaking up the sun at the beach—always with SPF, of course!

Specialties:

Laser Treatments, Microneedling, Medical-Grade Facials, Skincare Consultations

Marisa Kokoszka

Care Coordinator & Skincare Specialist

Marisa is a dedicated skincare specialist with a passion for enhancing natural beauty through expert techniques and personalized care. With an eye for creativity in the world of esthetics, she combines artistry with science to deliver results that leave clients glowing inside and out.

Specializing in brow artistry, Marisa has been helping others find their perfect brows for over five years. Her keen attention to detail and deep understanding of facial symmetry allow her to craft brows that complement each client's unique features. Whether shaping, tinting, or perfecting a natural arch, she believes that well designed brows can transform a face and boost confidence.

With a commitment to skin health and a creative approach to beauty, Marisa curates customized skincare routines and transformative experiences tailored to each individual. Her goal is to empower others to feel radiant, confident, and comfortable in their own skin.

Specialties:

Brow Shaping, Tinting & Lamination, Skincare Routines, Facial Symmetry Analysis
